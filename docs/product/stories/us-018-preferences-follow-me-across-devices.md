# US-018 — Preferences follow me across devices

Epic: [EP-03 — Preferences, language & theme](../epics/ep-03-preferences-language-theme.md)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Language and theme (`light` / `dark` / `system`) are persisted server-side per user
